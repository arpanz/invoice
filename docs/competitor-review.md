Where we are level or ahead: GST (CGST/SGST/IGST, HSN), UPI QR on PDF, partial payments with history, estimates → invoice, signature, deep PDF customiser, dark mode, JSON backup, no forced sign-up, no hard paywall at launch.

## Things they do better in how it works

- **InvoiceBase** puts you straight into your first invoice after onboarding. Business, client and items are filled inline, not in setup forms first.
- **Simple Invoice Manager** asks "Apply India settings?" once and sets currency, date format, financial year, amount-in-words, GST and HSN together.
- **Easy Invoice** onboarding: language → currency (with a live preview of how amounts look) → business (logo + payment QR) → "what will you use it for?" (estimate / quotation / invoice / receipt toggles). Short, and it ends with a personalised home ("Good afternoon 👋", billed / paid / outstanding).
- **InvoiceBase** invoice settings has simple PDF toggles: show balance due, PAID stamp, total quantity, qty and unit shown apart.
- Dashboards use date chips (Today / Week / Month / Year / All time) with filters by client and item.

## Pricing seen
InvoiceBase: ₹1,000/yr (₹15.99 first-week trial), ₹250/mo, ₹1,500 lifetime, with a hard paywall shown during onboarding (it can be closed).
Invoice Simple: tiers of 3, 10 or unlimited invoices a month. Photos, signature and reminders are in the higher tiers.
Our free tier (10 invoices a month, all designs free) is already generous next to these.

## Suggested priority (biggest gap for the effort)
1. Fix attachments (save them, show them in the PDF). This is a bug, not a missing feature.
2. Receipts plus a PAID stamp. Both build on the payments we already store.
3. Reports screen: date-range chips, revenue trend, top clients and items, CSV export.
4. Payment-due reminders as local notifications, plus a one-tap WhatsApp reminder message to the client.
5. Number prefix and custom fields.
6. India quick-setup and first-invoice-first onboarding.
7. More templates (aim for 25–30, grouped by industry).
8. Hindi (and other) app languages, and app lock.
9. Later: expenses, credit notes / proforma, recurring invoices, thermal printing.
