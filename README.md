# Invoice Maker Pro

An offline invoice and estimate app for small businesses and freelancers, built with Flutter. Everything is stored on the device; no account is needed.

Android package: `com.livinlabs.invoice`.

---

## What it does

- **Invoices**: create, edit, duplicate, delete. Line items with quantity, unit, HSN/SAC, per-line discount and tax. Document discount, shipping, GST split (CGST/SGST or IGST) or a single tax for other countries.
- **Payments**: each invoice keeps a payment history (date, amount, method, note). The paid amount is the sum of its payments, and the status (Unpaid, Partially Paid, Paid) follows from it. Overdue is worked out from the due date, never stored. "Sent" is recorded when the user confirms after sharing.
- **Estimates**: create, share, and convert to an invoice once. A converted estimate is locked.
- **Clients and Items**: saved clients with their invoice totals; a catalogue of saved items with categories.
- **PDF**: several templates and a design studio. Thousands separators, per-rate tax rows, page numbers on long documents, and the amount in words for INR. Fonts for Indian scripts (Devanagari, Tamil, Telugu and others) download the first time a document needs them and are kept on the device.
- **Appearance**: light and dark themes, following the device by default (Settings → Appearance).
- **Export**: share, print, or save the PDF to Downloads (Android) or the Files app (iOS). Settings → Export Data writes a JSON backup (it cannot be restored in the app yet).

## Free and Pro

| | Free | Pro |
|---|---|---|
| Invoices | 10 per calendar month | Unlimited |
| Estimates | 10 per calendar month | Unlimited |
| Saved clients | 10 | Unlimited |
| PDF watermark | "Generated by Invoice Maker Pro" | None |

Pro is sold as in-app purchases: `invoice_pro_lifetime` (one-time), `invoice_pro_yearly` and `invoice_pro_monthly` (subscriptions). The older `invoice_maker_pro_lifetime` product is still honoured for restores. The limits live in [`lib/core/billing/free_limits.dart`](lib/core/billing/free_limits.dart).

The app has no ads.

## Project layout

```
lib/
├── core/
│   ├── app/          # review prompt, update check
│   ├── billing/      # in-app purchases, free-plan limits
│   ├── database/     # SQLite schema (v6), migrations, transactional saves
│   ├── models/       # currencies and tax setup per country
│   ├── providers/    # app-wide currency
│   ├── theme/        # design tokens (light and dark), theme switching, animations
│   └── utils/        # totals, number parsing, file export, images
├── features/
│   ├── dashboard/    # Invoices tab
│   ├── invoices/     # editor, preview, payments, PDF rendering
│   ├── estimates/
│   ├── clients/
│   ├── items/
│   ├── onboarding/
│   ├── paywall/
│   └── settings/
├── shared_widgets/
└── main.dart         # start-up migrations and the tab shell
```

Key files:

| File | Purpose |
|---|---|
| [`lib/core/utils/document_totals.dart`](lib/core/utils/document_totals.dart) | The one totals calculation used by the editor, the PDF and the lists |
| [`lib/core/database/db_provider.dart`](lib/core/database/db_provider.dart) | Schema, migrations, transactional saves and numbering |
| [`lib/features/invoices/services/pdf_generator_service.dart`](lib/features/invoices/services/pdf_generator_service.dart) | PDF layout |
| [`lib/features/invoices/services/invoice_render_service.dart`](lib/features/invoices/services/invoice_render_service.dart) | Chooses the business details and design for a saved invoice |
| [`lib/core/billing/billing_service.dart`](lib/core/billing/billing_service.dart) | Purchases and restores |
